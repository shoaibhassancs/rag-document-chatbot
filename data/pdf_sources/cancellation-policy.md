# Cancellation and Missed Appointment Policy

Effective January 1, 2026

## Why we have this policy

Every appointment at BrightSmile Dental is reserved for you alone. When a time slot goes unused at short notice, we cannot offer it to another patient, and the clinicians and assistants who prepared for your visit are still paid. This policy keeps our schedule fair for everyone, including patients waiting for an earlier appointment.

## How much notice we need

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule any appointment. Notice given 24 hours or more before your appointment time is free of charge, however many times you reschedule.

## Cancellation fees

Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice is a late cancellation. A same-day cancellation is always a late cancellation. A missed appointment (a no-show) is charged exactly the same as a late cancellation.

- Standard appointments (up to 60 minutes, such as exams, cleanings, X-rays and fillings): $50
- Extended appointments (longer than 60 minutes, such as crowns, root canals, wisdom tooth removal, implant surgery and any visit with sedation): $150

So if you cancel a standard appointment on the same day, the fee is $50. If you cancel an extended appointment on the same day, the fee is $150. Appointments with oral sedation need 48 hours' notice instead of 24, and the $150 fee applies if you give less.

Dental insurance does not pay cancellation fees. The fee is the patient's responsibility.

## How to cancel or reschedule

- Call the front desk on (555) 014-2200 during opening hours.
- Email hello@brightsmile-dental.example at any time. We read email at the start of each working day, and the time your email arrives is your time of notice.
- Leave a voicemail outside opening hours. The time of your voicemail is your time of notice.

Cancelling by text reply is not supported, because a reply to an appointment reminder is not monitored by a person.

## When we waive the fee

We will waive the fee when:

- you had a dental or medical emergency, or you were admitted to hospital;
- you were ill and can provide a note from your doctor within 7 days;
- a death or serious illness in your immediate family made it impossible to attend;
- severe weather or a road closure made travel unsafe.

We also offer one courtesy waiver in any 12-month period for patients who simply forgot or had a scheduling accident. Ask the front desk when you call. A courtesy waiver applies to the first late cancellation or no-show only.

## Repeated missed appointments

If you have three late cancellations or no-shows within 12 months, we will ask you to keep a card on file before booking any further appointments. After a fourth, we may ask you to pay for extended appointments in advance. We will always tell you before this applies and we will explain why.

## Arriving late

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the clinician may not have enough time to complete your treatment safely. In that case we will offer the next available time and the original appointment is treated as a late cancellation, unless you called ahead to tell us you were running late.

## Children and guardians

The same policy applies to children's appointments. The parent or guardian who booked the appointment is responsible for any fee.

## How fees are collected

Fees are charged to the card on file, or added to your next statement. A fee must be paid before your next appointment can begin. If you believe a fee was charged in error, tell us within 30 days and the practice manager will review it.

## Our commitment to you

If we need to cancel or move your appointment, we will tell you as early as we can and offer the first suitable time. We never charge a fee when the clinic cancels.
