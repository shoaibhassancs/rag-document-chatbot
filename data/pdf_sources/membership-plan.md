# BrightSmile Smile Club

Our in-house membership plan for patients without dental insurance. Effective January 1, 2026

## What is the Smile Club?

The Smile Club is a simple yearly plan. You pay one fee, and your regular preventive care and a discount on other treatment are covered. It is not insurance. There are no claim forms, no deductibles, no annual maximums and no waiting periods. You can use your benefits from the day you join.

## Membership fees

- Adult (13 and over), $29 per month or $299 per year
- Child (under 13), $19 per month or $199 per year
- Family of three or more members: 10 percent off the total membership fee

The yearly option saves $49 per adult compared with paying monthly. Monthly fees are paid by card on the same date each month.

## What is included

Each adult member receives, every membership year:

- two periodic exams;
- two adult cleanings;
- one set of bitewing X-rays;
- one emergency exam, with no fee;
- one set of in-office fluoride treatment if your dentist recommends it.

Each child member receives:

- two exams and two cleanings;
- one set of bitewing X-rays, when the dentist recommends them;
- two fluoride varnish treatments;
- one custom sports mouthguard, if your child plays contact sports.

A full-mouth or panoramic X-ray, a new patient exam and a deep cleaning are not part of the plan, but members receive the discount on them.

## Discount on other treatment

Members receive 15 percent off the prices on our Services and Prices list for treatment that the plan does not include. This covers fillings, crowns, root canals, extractions, whitening, night guards and dentures.

The discount does not apply to:

- orthodontic treatment and clear aligners;
- dental implants;
- laboratory fees from outside suppliers;
- the cost of sedation;
- any treatment already paid for or begun before you joined.

You cannot combine the discount with insurance or with any other offer.

## Who can join

Anyone can join, with or without insurance, but the plan is designed for people with none. If you have dental insurance, it will probably give you better value, so we recommend you compare before you join. Ask the front desk to compare for you. We are glad to help.

## How to join and renew

- Ask at the front desk or call (555) 014-2200.
- Complete a short form and pay your first monthly or yearly fee.
- Your membership year starts on the day you join.
- Yearly plans renew automatically on the anniversary, and we email you 30 days before. You can cancel at any time before that date.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice. If you pay monthly, you pay for the notice period. If you pay yearly, you may use the benefits until the end of the year but we do not refund the fee for unused months, unless we have cancelled the plan.

## Unused benefits

Benefits do not roll over. If you do not use both cleanings in a membership year, the unused cleaning is lost at renewal. We will remind you in the last three months of your year.

## Using your benefits

Book as usual and tell the front desk you are a member. We will confirm your benefits when you arrive. Included visits have no fee. Any other treatment is charged at the member price, which is shown on your treatment plan.

## Late payments

If a monthly payment fails, we will contact you. If it is not paid within 30 days, your membership is paused until the balance is cleared, and you pay full prices until then.

## Moving or transferring

Memberships belong to the person named on the plan and cannot be transferred. If you move away, you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

## Policy of fairness

If we change the fees or benefits, we will tell you at least 60 days before the change, and the new terms apply from your next renewal.
