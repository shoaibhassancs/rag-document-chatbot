# Patient Welcome Guide

Welcome to BrightSmile Dental

## Who we are

BrightSmile Dental is a family dental practice in Riverton, Ohio. Dr. Maya Patel opened the clinic in 2011 with a simple aim: dentistry that is careful, clear and comfortable. Today a team of three dentists, two hygienists and six support staff cares for patients from toddlers to grandparents.

We believe good dental care starts with good communication. You will always be told what we found, what your options are and what each option costs, before anything is done.

## What to expect at your first visit

Your first visit takes about 75 minutes. It gives us a complete picture of your oral health.

- A friendly check-in, and a short review of the forms you completed online.
- A conversation about your concerns, your goals and any worries about dental treatment.
- A review of your medical history, medicines and allergies.
- Digital X-rays, so we can see what cannot be seen from the surface.
- A thorough examination of your teeth, gums, jaw joints, tongue and the soft tissues of your mouth.
- An oral cancer screening, which takes about a minute and is painless.
- A professional cleaning, when your gums are healthy enough on the day.
- A clear, written treatment plan in plain language, with priorities and costs.

If your gums need more than a standard cleaning, we will explain why and plan a separate deep cleaning visit rather than rushing it.

## Our approach to comfort

Many people feel anxious about dental visits, and we take that seriously. You are always in control of your visit.

- Tell us at any time to stop, and we will stop. Most patients agree a simple hand signal with the dentist at the start.
- We explain each step before we do it, so there are no surprises.
- We offer blankets, neck pillows, noise-cancelling headphones and TV screens on the ceiling.
- Numbing gel is applied before any injection, and we inject slowly.
- Our Sedation Options guide describes gentle ways to relax, from laughing gas to oral sedation.

## Technology that helps you

- Digital X-rays use up to 80 percent less radiation than traditional film X-rays and appear on the screen immediately.
- An intra-oral camera lets you see for yourself what the dentist sees.
- A digital scanner takes impressions without the gooey trays.
- Tooth-colored fillings blend with your natural teeth.

## Your treatment plan and consent

After your exam, your dentist presents your treatment plan. It lists each recommended treatment, its priority, its cost and what your insurance is expected to pay. Nothing is done until you agree. You are welcome to take the plan home, to ask for a second opinion elsewhere, or to start with the most urgent items and do the rest later.

## Bringing a family member

You are welcome to bring a family member or friend into the treatment room for support. For children under 13 an adult must stay in the clinic for the whole appointment. Our Children's Dentistry guide explains how we work with young patients.

## Forms and records

You can complete your new patient forms online before your visit, which saves about 15 minutes. If you cannot, please allow extra time to fill them in on paper at the clinic.

We can request your previous dental records and X-rays from your old dentist. Ask the front desk and sign a records release form.

## Accessibility

Our clinic is on the ground floor with step-free access, wide doorways and an accessible treatment room. If you use a wheelchair, a hearing aid or a service animal, or if you have any other need that would make your visit easier, tell us when you book and we will plan for it. We can also provide written instructions in large print.

## Your rights as a patient

You have the right to:

- be treated with courtesy and respect;
- understand your diagnosis, your options and the cost of each;
- say no to any treatment, or to ask for a different one;
- receive a copy of your records;
- have your information kept private, as described in our Privacy Notice;
- make a complaint and have it taken seriously, using our Complaints Procedure.

## Our responsibilities and yours

We promise to explain, to listen and to keep our clinic clean and safe. We ask you to tell us about any change in your health or medicines, to arrive on time, to let us know in good time if you need to change an appointment and to follow the care instructions we give you.

## Looking after your smile between visits

Healthy teeth depend on what happens at home. Our Dental Hygiene Tips guide explains brushing, flossing and diet. After specific treatments, we give you a printed aftercare sheet, and the same instructions are in our aftercare guides.

## Staying in touch

We send appointment reminders by text and email. See our Appointment Reminders note for how that works. For anything else, the front desk is happy to help by phone or email. We aim to reply to every email within one working day.

## Thank you

Thank you for choosing BrightSmile Dental. We look forward to meeting you and to helping you keep your smile healthy for life.
