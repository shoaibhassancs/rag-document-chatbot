# Privacy Notice

How BrightSmile Dental uses your information. Effective January 1, 2026

## Who we are

BrightSmile Dental, 214 Maple Avenue, Suite 3, Riverton, OH 43001, is responsible for the information you give us. Our privacy officer is Grace Whitfield. You can reach her at privacy@brightsmile-dental.example or on (555) 014-2200.

## What information we collect

- Your name, date of birth, address, phone number and email address.
- Your medical and dental history, medicines and allergies.
- Your insurance details.
- Your X-rays, photographs, scans, examination notes and treatment records.
- Records of your appointments, payments and any messages you send us.
- Information from your previous dentist, when you ask us to request your records.
- Technical information from our website, such as the pages visited and your browser type.

## Why we use your information

We use your information to:

- provide your dental care and keep an accurate record of it;
- book appointments and send reminders;
- bill you and your insurance company;
- meet legal and professional obligations;
- improve our service, for example by checking that our systems work.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your health information for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share information only when we need to.

- Your insurer, to process claims you ask us to make.
- A specialist, laboratory or dental technician involved in your care. We send only what they need.
- A payment processor, when you pay by card.
- Our software suppliers, who store data on our behalf and are bound by contract to protect it.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.
- Another person, only with your written permission. This includes a spouse or parent, except where the law gives a parent the right to a child's records.

## How long we keep records

- Adult records: 10 years after your last visit.
- Children's records: until the child is 21, or for 10 years after the last visit, whichever is longer.
- Financial records: 7 years.
- Records of a patient who asks us to delete their information: we remove what the law allows and keep what the law requires us to keep.

After that, records are securely destroyed.

## How we keep information safe

- Records are stored on encrypted systems, and only authorized staff can open them.
- Staff sign a confidentiality agreement and receive privacy training every year.
- We keep a log of who looks at each record.
- Paper records are locked away and shredded when no longer needed.
- If there is a breach that could harm you, we will tell you promptly, and tell the authorities where the law requires.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- see your records and receive a copy. We respond within 30 days and may charge a reasonable copying fee;
- ask us to correct information that is wrong;
- ask us to limit how we use your information in some situations;
- ask for a list of times we have shared your information, apart from sharing for your care, payment and routine operations;
- choose how we contact you, for example by email only;
- stop appointment reminders and marketing messages at any time;
- make a complaint to us or to the authorities.

To use any of these rights, write to the privacy officer. We may ask you to prove your identity first, to protect your privacy.

## Reminders and marketing

We send appointment reminders by text and email because they are part of your care. We send marketing messages, such as a newsletter, only if you have agreed, and each one tells you how to stop.

## Our website

Our website uses a small number of cookies to make pages work and to count visits. It does not collect your health information. You can turn cookies off in your browser. A form you send through the website goes to our team by email, so please do not put sensitive health details in it.

## Children

For patients under 18, a parent or legal guardian gives consent and receives information on the child's behalf. We follow the law on what young people may decide for themselves.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed. We keep a copy at the front desk and on our website.
