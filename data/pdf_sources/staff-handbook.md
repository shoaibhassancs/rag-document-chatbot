# BrightSmile Dental Staff Handbook

For employees only. Version 4, January 2026

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the team. This handbook explains how we work together and what you can expect from us. It is not a contract. If something here conflicts with your employment agreement, your agreement applies. Ask Grace Whitfield, the office manager, if you have any questions.

Our values are care, clarity and respect. We care for patients and for each other. We explain things clearly, and we treat everybody with respect, whatever their role.

## 2. Roles and who to ask

- Dr. Maya Patel, owner and principal dentist: clinical standards and final decisions on treatment policy.
- Dr. Daniel Okafor and Dr. Lena Brandt: associate dentists.
- Priya Nair and Tom Alvarez: dental hygienists.
- Grace Whitfield: office manager, payroll, hiring, privacy officer and first contact for any staff concern.
- Dental assistants and front desk team: report to Grace for scheduling and to the dentist you assist for clinical matters.

## 3. Working hours and attendance

- Staff arrive 30 minutes before the first patient. The clinic opens to patients at 8:00 am Monday to Friday, so clinical staff start at 7:30 am.
- Please clock in and out using the time system at the staff entrance.
- If you will be late or cannot attend, phone Grace before 7:00 am. Do not send a text message only.
- We schedule one 30-minute unpaid lunch break on each full working day. Breaks are taken in the staff room, not in patient areas.
- Saturday shifts are rotated. The rota is published on the first of each month.

## 4. Time off

- Paid time off (PTO) accrues at 10 days per year in your first three years and 15 days per year after that.
- Sick leave is 5 paid days per year. After 3 consecutive sick days, please give a doctor's note.
- Request PTO at least 2 weeks in advance, and 6 weeks for a week or longer, using the shared calendar. Requests are approved on a first-come basis, with at least one hygienist and one front desk team member always working.
- The clinic is closed on the public holidays listed in our Holiday Closures notice. Those days are paid for full-time staff.
- Bereavement leave is up to 3 paid days for an immediate family member.

## 5. Pay and benefits

- Pay day is every second Friday. Pay slips are emailed.
- Health insurance starts after 60 days of employment. The clinic pays 70 percent of the premium for employees.
- A retirement plan is available after 6 months, and the clinic matches your contributions up to 3 percent of pay.
- Staff and their dependents receive 50 percent off treatment at BrightSmile Dental, excluding laboratory costs.
- A continuing education allowance of $750 per year is available to clinical staff. Get approval from Grace before you book a course.
- A uniform allowance of $120 per year is paid in January.

## 6. Dress code and appearance

- Wear the navy scrubs issued to you, clean and ironed, with the clinic name badge visible.
- Closed-toe, non-slip shoes only.
- Keep long hair tied back. Keep nails short and clean, with no artificial nails or nail polish in clinical areas.
- Avoid strong perfume and aftershave. Some patients are sensitive to fragrance.
- Jewellery should be minimal in clinical areas. Rings that cannot be worn under gloves must be removed.

## 7. Patient service standards

- Answer the phone within three rings. Say: "BrightSmile Dental, this is [name], how can I help?"
- Greet every patient who walks in within one minute, even if you are on the phone.
- Offer a clear next step on every call: a booking, a call back by a stated time or a transfer.
- Never guess. If you do not know an answer, say that you will find out and call back, then do so the same day.
- Do not discuss prices or insurance estimates beyond the printed price list. Hand complex insurance questions to the billing team.
- Be patient with anxious patients. Offer water, a blanket and an explanation before treatment.

## 8. Handling complaints

Treat every complaint calmly and say thank you for raising it. Listen without interrupting and do not argue or make excuses. Write down the details and tell the patient who will contact them and when. Pass every complaint to Grace the same day. The full steps are in the Complaints Procedure, which staff and patients both follow. Never promise a refund or a waiver of fees yourself. Only Grace or Dr. Patel can decide those.

## 9. Confidentiality and data protection

- Patient information is confidential at all times, including after you leave the clinic.
- Only look at the records you need for your job. Looking up the record of a friend, a family member or a celebrity without a work reason is a serious breach.
- Do not discuss patients in the waiting room, in the corridor or on social media.
- Never leave a screen with patient information showing. Lock your computer when you step away.
- Do not take photos of patients or of treatment without written consent and Dr. Patel's approval.
- Paper records with patient details go in the locked shredding bin.
- Report any suspected breach to Grace immediately.

## 10. Infection control duties

Everyone who works in clinical areas must follow our infection control procedures at all times. They are set out in the Infection Control document, and we review them every year. In summary: wash and sanitize your hands, wear the right protection, never skip sterilization steps and report any needle-stick injury immediately. Training is repeated every year in February.

## 11. Safety and emergencies

- Fire exits are at the front entrance, the staff entrance and the rear door of the sterilization room. The assembly point is the far end of the car park.
- Fire drills take place twice a year.
- The first aid kit is in the staff room, and the emergency oxygen and the automated external defibrillator (AED) are in the corridor next to the sterilization room.
- If a patient has a medical emergency, call 911 first, then tell Dr. Patel or the nearest dentist.
- Staff trained in CPR are listed on the notice board and refreshed every two years.

## 12. Social media and communication

Do not post about patients or treatments. Do not speak for the clinic on social media or to journalists. Refer enquiries from the press to Dr. Patel. You are welcome to share the clinic's own posts.

## 13. Performance and development

- Every employee has a performance conversation with Grace each January, and a short check-in at 90 days after joining.
- We aim to promote from within whenever we can. Tell Grace if you want to train for a different role.
- If there is a performance concern, we will talk to you early and clearly, and give you a fair chance to improve.

## 14. Ending employment

We ask for 4 weeks' written notice when you resign. Please return your badge, keys and uniform on your last day. We will give you a final pay slip within one pay cycle.
