APPOINTMENT REMINDERS: HOW THEY WORK

We send reminders by text message and email so that you do not forget your visit.

WHEN YOU WILL HEAR FROM US

- 7 days before your appointment: an email with the date, time, dentist and any preparation, such as fasting or bringing a driver.
- 2 days before: a text message and an email asking you to confirm.
- On the morning of your appointment: a short text with the time and the address.

HOW TO CONFIRM

Reply "Y" to the text message, or click the link in the email, to confirm your visit. If we do not hear from you, we keep your appointment and may call you the day before.

IMPORTANT: reminder texts are sent from an automated number. Messages sent to it are not read by the team, so you cannot cancel or change an appointment by replying to a reminder. To change an appointment, call (555) 014-2200 or email hello@brightsmile-dental.example. See the Cancellation Policy for notice times.

STOPPING REMINDERS

Reply "STOP" to any text message to stop text reminders. Contact the front desk to stop email reminders. Stopping reminders does not cancel your appointment, and a missed appointment is treated as described in the Cancellation Policy.

CHANGING YOUR CONTACT DETAILS

Please tell the front desk if your mobile number or email changes, so that reminders reach you. We use these details only for messages about your care, as described in the Privacy Notice.

RECALL REMINDERS

If you are due for a 6-month check-up and have not booked, we will send you a reminder by email and, if you prefer, by post. We send up to 3 recall reminders, each about 2 weeks apart.

CHILDREN

For a child, reminders go to the parent or guardian listed on the account.

TEXT MESSAGE COSTS

We do not charge for reminders. Your phone company may charge for text messages under your plan.
