About BrightSmile Dental

Dr. Maya Patel founded BrightSmile Dental in 2011. We are a team of three dentists, two hygienists and six support staff who care for patients from their first tooth to their golden years.

We believe in explaining everything before we do it, and in giving you a written plan and a clear price before treatment.

What we do

  • Check-ups, cleanings and X-rays for adults and children
  • Fillings, crowns, root canals and extractions
  • Teeth whitening, clear aligners and braces
  • Nitrous oxide and oral sedation for anxious patients
  • Same-day emergency appointments

Our fees are on the Services and Prices list, and we accept a range of insurance plans. We never use silver amalgam fillings.

Smile Rewards: refer a friend

Tell a friend about BrightSmile Dental. When a friend you refer completes their first visit with us, you each receive a $25 credit on your account.

  • There is no limit to the number of friends you can refer.
  • Credits are applied to your next visit and last for 12 months.
  • The credit cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Ask the front desk for a referral card, or mention your name when your friend books.

In the community

Every October we run Free Smile Day for local families, with free check-ups for children under 12 and a school toothbrush drive. Every spring, Dr. Brandt visits Riverton primary schools to teach children how to brush.
