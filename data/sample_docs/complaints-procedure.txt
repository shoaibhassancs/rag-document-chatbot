COMPLAINTS PROCEDURE

We want to hear when something has not gone well. A complaint helps us improve, and you will not be treated differently for making one.

HOW TO COMPLAIN

You can complain in any of these ways:

- speak to any team member, who will pass it on to the office manager;
- call (555) 014-2200 and ask for Grace Whitfield, the office manager;
- email hello@brightsmile-dental.example with "Complaint" in the subject line;
- write to: Office Manager, BrightSmile Dental, 214 Maple Avenue, Suite 3, Riverton, OH 43001.

Please give your name, the date of your visit, what happened and what you would like us to do. You may ask someone to complain on your behalf.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

1. We acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days.
2. Grace Whitfield records it and, where it concerns treatment, asks the dentist involved for their account.
3. We may invite you to talk by phone or in person, with a companion if you like.
4. We give you a written reply within 15 working days. It explains what we found, what we will do and, where we have made a mistake, an apology.
5. If we need more time, we will tell you why and when you can expect an answer.

OUR PROMISES

- We will take your complaint seriously and treat it as confidential.
- We will look into it fairly.
- We will not charge you for the time we spend on it.
- We will make changes where we find something needs to change.

IF YOU ARE NOT SATISFIED

If you are not happy with our reply, you can ask Dr. Maya Patel, the principal dentist, to review it. She will reply within 10 working days.

You may also contact the State Dental Board of Ohio, which handles concerns about the professional conduct of dentists. Contacting the Board does not stop you from complaining to us as well.

REFUNDS AND WAIVERS

Only the office manager or the principal dentist can decide on a refund or the waiver of a fee. Other team members cannot promise one.

PATIENT FEEDBACK

We read all the feedback we receive, good and bad, at our monthly team meeting. Thank you for helping us to get better.
