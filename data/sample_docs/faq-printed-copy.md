# BrightSmile Dental: Frequently Asked Questions



## Visits and appointments



### How long does a routine check-up and cleaning take?


Plan on 45 to 60 minutes. A first visit takes about 75 minutes because it includes X-rays and a full examination.  


### Do you see walk-in patients?


We prefer appointments, so that we can give each patient the time they need. If you are in pain, call us and we will find a time on the same day.  


### Can I choose my dentist?


Yes. Tell the front desk whether you would like Dr. Patel, Dr. Okafor or Dr. Brandt when you book. Dr. Brandt sees children and families, and Dr. Okafor is our root canal specialist.  


### Can I book online?


You can request an appointment through our website, and a member of the team will call you to confirm it. You can also call us or email.  


### Can someone come with me to my appointment?


Yes. A friend or family member is welcome in the treatment room or the waiting area.  


## Your health and comfort



### I am nervous about the dentist. Can you help?


Yes. Many of our patients feel the same. Tell us when you book, and we will plan a slower visit with extra explanation and breaks. Our Sedation Options guide lists the other ways we can help you relax.  


### Are dental X-rays safe?


Our digital X-rays use very low doses of radiation, far less than film X-rays. We take only the X-rays you need, and we use a lead apron and a thyroid collar for every patient.  


### I am pregnant. Can I still have dental treatment?


Yes. Routine cleanings and exams are safe and important during pregnancy, because hormonal changes can affect your gums. Please tell us you are pregnant when you book. We usually postpone elective treatment and some X-rays until after the baby is born, and we will discuss your options.  


### Do I need to tell you about my medicines?


Yes. Please bring a list of all your prescription medicines and supplements to your first visit, and tell us about any change. Some medicines affect dental treatment.  


### Do you offer a second opinion?


Yes. You are welcome to bring a treatment plan from another dentist and we will give you our honest view.

Please see the Location and Parking guide.  


### Can I get my records or X-rays sent to another dentist?


Yes. Sign a records release form at the front desk, or email us. We usually send records within 5 working days.  


## Looking after your teeth



### How often should I visit the dentist?


Most people should have a check-up and cleaning every 6 months. If you have gum disease or a higher risk of decay, we may suggest every 3 or 4 months.  


### What should I do if I have a toothache at night?


If it is a dental emergency and you are an existing patient, please read our Emergency Dental Care guide for the right number to call.  


### My gums bleed when I brush. Is that normal?


Bleeding is usually a sign of gum inflammation caused by plaque. It is not a reason to stop brushing. Brush and floss gently and regularly, and book a check-up if it continues for more than two weeks.
