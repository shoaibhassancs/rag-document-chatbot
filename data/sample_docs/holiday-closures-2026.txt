NOTES

1. Saturdays that fall in a holiday weekend are open as usual unless listed above.

3. Please book follow-up visits for the week before a long closure if your treatment is time-sensitive, such as a temporary crown.
4. Closed days are paid days for full-time staff.
5. The annual staff training day is on Friday, February 13, 2026. The clinic opens at 12:00 noon on that day.
