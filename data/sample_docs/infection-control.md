# Infection Control at BrightSmile Dental

Your safety is the foundation of everything we do. Our procedures follow the guidance of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). We review them every year.

## Instruments and sterilization

- Every reusable instrument is cleaned, rinsed, wrapped and sterilized in an autoclave, which uses steam under pressure, after every patient.
- Instruments stay sealed in their sterilization pouches until your dentist opens them in front of you.
- We test our autoclaves every week with a biological spore test, which uses living spores to prove the machine kills germs. Results are logged and kept.
- Our sterilization technician, Elena Ortiz, keeps these records and checks the machines every day.
- Drill bits (burs), needles, syringes, saliva ejector tips, prophy cups and other items are single-use, and we throw them away after your visit.

## Hand hygiene and protection

- Staff wash or sanitize their hands before and after each patient.
- Clinicians wear a new pair of gloves and a fresh mask for every patient, and eye protection or a face shield. Gowns are worn for procedures that create spray.
- Patients are given protective glasses.
- A staff member with a fever, a cough or a stomach illness stays at home.

## Surfaces and treatment rooms

- After each patient, every surface that was touched is cleaned and disinfected with a hospital-grade disinfectant, including the chair, light handles, trays, counters and the computer keyboard.
- Surfaces that are hard to clean, such as light handles and chair controls, are covered with disposable barriers that are replaced for each patient.
- Treatment rooms are ventilated, and high-efficiency air filters run in each room.

## Water lines

The tubes that deliver water to our handpieces are flushed for 2 minutes at the start of each day and for 20 seconds between patients. We treat the water lines each week and test the water quality every month.

## X-rays

Digital X-ray sensors are covered with a fresh disposable barrier for each patient and disinfected afterwards.

## Waste

Sharps go in puncture-proof containers, and clinical waste is collected by a licensed contractor. Amalgam is not used in our clinic, so there is none to dispose of.

## Staff health and training

- All clinical staff are offered vaccination against hepatitis B.
- Everyone who works in a clinical area completes infection control training every February, and again when procedures change.
- Any needle-stick or splash injury is reported at once and followed up according to our written protocol.

## Illness and your visit

If you are unwell, with a fever, a cough, or a sore throat, please call us and rearrange your appointment. Dental emergencies are the exception: call us and we will tell you how to come in safely.

## Questions

If you would like to see how we clean instruments or have any worry about infection control, ask any team member. We are happy to show you.
