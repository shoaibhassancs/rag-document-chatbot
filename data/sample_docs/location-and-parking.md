# Location and Parking

## Our address

BrightSmile Dental
214 Maple Avenue, Suite 3
Riverton, OH 43001

We are on the ground floor of the Maple Court building, next to the Riverton Library and across the road from Central Park.

## Parking

- Free parking is available in the lot behind the building, with 42 spaces. Enter from Second Street, not from Maple Avenue.
- Two spaces next to the back entrance are reserved for patients with disabilities. They are clearly marked.
- There are two electric vehicle charging points at the far end of the lot. Charging is free for patients during their appointment.
- Street parking on Maple Avenue is limited to 2 hours. Please check the signs.
- The lot is lit and covered by cameras. It is not reserved for patients only, so allow a few extra minutes at busy times.

There is no charge for parking, and we do not validate parking tickets, because we do not have any.

## Directions

- From the highway: take Exit 12 (Riverton), follow Main Street for 1.2 miles and turn left onto Maple Avenue. We are on the right, after the library.
- From downtown Riverton: head north on Second Street, turn right at Maple Avenue and the clinic is on your left.

## Public transport

Bus route 14 stops at the corner of Maple Avenue and Second Street, a 2-minute walk from our door. Buses run about every 20 minutes on weekdays and every 40 minutes on Saturdays. The nearest bus stop on the return route is outside the library.

## Cycling and walking

There is a bike rack beside the front entrance. The riverside path connects to Maple Avenue and takes about 10 minutes from the town centre.

## Getting into the building

- The front entrance has a level threshold and an automatic door.
- The back entrance next to the car park also has step-free access.
- Inside, all treatment rooms are on the same floor. There is a wheelchair-accessible restroom beside the waiting room.

## Finding us

Our sign is on the front of the building in green and white. If you use a satnav, search for "214 Maple Avenue, Riverton". Call (555) 014-2200 if you cannot find us.
