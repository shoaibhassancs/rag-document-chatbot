# Opening Hours

## Regular hours

BrightSmile Dental is open at these times:

- Monday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm
- Tuesday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm
- Wednesday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm
- Thursday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm
- Friday: 8:00 am to 2:00 pm
- Saturday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm
- Sunday: closed

We are open on Saturdays, from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. Saturday appointments fill up quickly, so please book early if you would like one.

## Phone lines

The front desk answers the phone at (555) 014-2200 during opening hours. Outside opening hours, leave a voicemail and we will return your call by 10:00 am on the next working day. You can email hello@brightsmile-dental.example at any time.

## Out-of-hours emergencies

Existing patients can call the emergency line on (555) 014-2299 at any hour. See the Emergency Dental Care guide.

## Public holidays and special closures

We close on public holidays and on a few other days. The dates for 2026 are in the Holiday Closures notice. On the days before some holidays, we close early.

## Last appointments

The last appointment of the day starts 45 minutes before closing time for a check-up or cleaning, and 90 minutes before closing time for longer treatments.
