# Orthodontics: Braces and Clear Aligners

## Straightening your teeth

Straight teeth are easier to clean and can improve your bite as well as your smile. We offer two kinds of treatment for teenagers and adults: clear aligners and traditional metal braces.

## Free consultation

The first orthodontic consultation is free. We look at your teeth and bite, talk about your goals and explain which treatment is likely to suit you. There is no obligation to go ahead. If you decide to start, we take records (X-rays, photographs and a digital scan), and the records fee is credited toward your treatment when you start within 90 days. The fees are on our price list.

## Clear aligners

Clear aligners are a series of thin, clear plastic trays that gently move your teeth. You change to a new tray about every two weeks.

- Best for mild to moderate crowding, gaps and bite problems.
- Almost invisible, and you can take them out to eat, brush and floss.
- You must wear them 22 hours a day for them to work.
- Dr. Patel plans and supervises all clear aligner treatment.
- Treatment usually takes 9 to 18 months.
- You visit us every 6 to 8 weeks for a quick check.

## Traditional metal braces

Metal braces use small brackets bonded to your teeth and a wire that is adjusted over time.

- Best for complex cases, and for patients who may not wear aligners reliably.
- Treatment usually takes 12 to 24 months.
- Adjustment visits take place every 4 to 6 weeks.
- You can choose the color of the small rubber bands at each visit.
- Braces are planned and fitted by Dr. Samuel Reyes, a board-certified orthodontist who visits us on the first Wednesday of every month. Your adjustments between his visits are handled by our own team.

## Retainers

After your teeth are straight, you will need retainers to hold them in place. Teeth tend to drift back if you stop wearing them. Most patients wear retainers full-time for 3 months and then at night, indefinitely. If you lose or break a retainer, call us at once, as teeth can move within days.

## Caring for braces

- Brush after every meal, and floss daily with a floss threader or water flosser.
- Avoid sticky, hard and chewy foods such as caramel, popcorn, nuts, hard candy and ice.
- Cut apples and raw carrots into small pieces.
- Wear a mouthguard for sports. We can fit one over your braces.
- If a bracket or wire comes loose, call us. If the wire is poking, cover it with orthodontic wax until you can see us.

## Caring for aligners

- Rinse them every time you take them out, and clean them twice a day with a soft brush and cool water. Do not use hot water, which can warp them.
- Only drink water while they are in.
- Keep them in their case when they are not in your mouth. Do not wrap them in a napkin.
- Keep your previous set of aligners as a backup.

## Does it hurt?

Your teeth will feel sore for 2 to 3 days after you get your braces or change to a new aligner. Soft foods and over-the-counter pain relief help. The soreness fades as your mouth gets used to it.
