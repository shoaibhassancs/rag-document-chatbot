# Payment Plans and Payment Methods

## How you can pay

We accept:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards
- debit cards
- HSA and FSA cards
- cash
- personal checks, made out to BrightSmile Dental
- bank transfers, with the details on your statement

We do not accept cryptocurrency, and we do not take payments by phone from people who are not the patient or the account holder.

Payment is due at the time of the visit, unless you are on a payment plan or the treatment is billed to your insurance company.

## Option 1: in-house payment plan

For treatment between $300 and $1,500, you can spread the cost with no interest and no fees.

- You pay 25 percent of the total on the first day of treatment.
- The balance is split into 3 equal monthly payments.
- Payments are taken automatically from a card that you keep on file.
- There is no credit check.

If a payment fails, we will call you. After 30 days of non-payment, the remaining balance becomes due in full.

## Option 2: FlexiPay financing

For larger treatment, we work with FlexiPay, an independent finance company. FlexiPay is not part of BrightSmile Dental.

- Treatment from $500 is eligible.
- Terms range from 6 to 24 months.
- Approved applicants get 0 percent interest for the first 12 months. After that, a fixed rate set by FlexiPay applies and is shown in your offer.
- You apply online or in the clinic. It takes about 5 minutes and you get an answer straight away.
- FlexiPay makes the credit decision. We do not see the details, and being declined does not affect your care with us.

Read the FlexiPay terms carefully before you accept. Interest charged after the promotional period is high if you do not pay off the balance in time.

## HSA and FSA

You can use a health savings account (HSA) or a flexible spending account (FSA) card for any treatment, deductibles and co-payments. We provide itemized receipts for reimbursement. Many FSAs have a use-it-or-lose-it deadline, so ask us in the autumn if you would like to schedule treatment before the end of the year.

## Insurance and your share

If you have insurance, we collect your estimated share at the visit, as described in the Insurance and Billing Guide. A payment plan can cover that share as well.

## Membership as an alternative

If you do not have insurance, our Smile Club membership may cost less than paying standard prices. See the Membership Plan guide.

## Talking to us early

If paying is difficult, please tell us before treatment starts. We would much rather find a solution with you than have a bill become a worry. Ask for Jamal Carter at the front desk, who handles payments and payment plans, on (555) 014-2200.

## Getting a receipt

You can ask for a receipt at any time. We will email it to you.
