Our own Dr. Daniel Okafor performs most root canals. We refer to a specialist endodontist only for very complex cases, such as a tooth with unusual root shape or a previous failed root canal that needs a microscope.

HOW REFERRALS WORK

1. Your dentist explains why a specialist is needed and what to expect.
2. The front desk sends your records and X-rays to the specialist. This takes about 2 working days.
3. You book directly with the specialist. Their fees and insurance acceptance are different from ours, so please ask them.
4. After treatment, the specialist sends us a report, and we continue your regular care.

We do not receive any payment for referring you.
