# Meet the BrightSmile Team

## Dentists

### Dr. Maya Patel, DDS: Principal dentist and founder
Dr. Patel founded BrightSmile Dental in 2011. She trained at the Ohio State University College of Dentistry and has more than 15 years of experience in general and cosmetic dentistry. She places the clinic's clear aligners and is happiest when a nervous patient leaves smiling. Outside the clinic she runs the Riverton 10K and bakes sourdough.

### Dr. Daniel Okafor, DDS: Associate dentist, root canal specialist
Dr. Okafor joined in 2016. He has completed advanced training in endodontics, the treatment of the inside of the tooth, and performs most of our root canals. Patients often say his calm voice made the difference. He speaks English and some Igbo, and he coaches a youth football team.

### Dr. Lena Brandt, DMD: Associate dentist, children and families
Dr. Brandt joined in 2019 after completing a pediatric dentistry residency. She sees our youngest patients and also treats parents and grandparents. She is known for her puppets and for turning a first visit into a game. Dr. Brandt speaks English and German.

## Hygienists

### Priya Nair, RDH
Priya has been a hygienist for 12 years. She teaches patients how to get the best from brushing and flossing, and she specializes in gum care. Priya speaks English and Hindi.

### Tom Alvarez, RDH
Tom is a hygienist with a gentle touch and a talent for helping anxious patients relax. He speaks English and Spanish and plays the guitar in a local band.
