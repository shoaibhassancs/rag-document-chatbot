WAITING ROOM NOTICE: PLEASE READ

Welcome to BrightSmile Dental. A few things help everyone feel comfortable while they wait.

WI-FI
Free guest Wi-Fi is available. The network name is BrightSmile-Guest. It has no password. Please do not use it for confidential banking or work.

PHONES AND NOISE
Please keep your phone on silent and take calls outside. Some patients are nervous, and a quiet room helps them.

CHILDREN
Please keep children with you in the waiting room. The play corner is for children under 10. We have books, a bead maze and colouring pages. Please clean up toys, and do not leave children alone.

FRAGRANCE-FREE CLINIC
Some patients and staff have asthma or allergies. Please avoid strong perfume, cologne and scented products when you visit.

FOOD AND DRINK
Water is free from the dispenser by the window. Please finish any hot drinks, food or chewing gum before you come in. Dental treatment is easier with a clean mouth.

REFRESHMENTS
We provide mints without sugar, after your visit.

WHEELCHAIRS AND PRAMS
There is space beside the reception desk for wheelchairs, walkers and prams. Ask a team member if you need help.

PRIVACY
When you check in, you may speak to the receptionist quietly, or ask to speak in the private area beside the desk. Please give other patients space at the desk.

LOST PROPERTY
Items left behind are kept at the front desk for 30 days.

SMOKING AND VAPING
The building and the car park are smoke-free and vape-free.

CAMERAS
Our entrance and car park have cameras for safety. There are no cameras in treatment rooms or restrooms.

COMPLIMENTS AND CONCERNS
We love to hear what we do well. If something was not right, please tell any team member or see our Complaints Procedure.

Thank you for helping us keep the clinic calm and welcoming.
