# After Teeth Whitening

## The first 48 hours: the "white diet"

After whitening, the surface of your teeth is more open and more easily stained. For the first 48 hours, eat and drink only light-colored foods and drinks.

Good choices:

- water, milk and clear drinks;
- white rice, pasta, potatoes and chicken;
- white fish and eggs;
- bananas, cauliflower and white bread;
- plain yogurt and white cheese.

Avoid:

- coffee, black tea, cola and red wine;
- tomato sauce, ketchup, soy sauce and curry;
- berries, beetroot and dark chocolate;
- colored sports drinks and popsicles;
- smoking and vaping.

If you cannot give up your coffee, drink it through a straw and rinse your mouth with water after.

## Sensitivity

Many people feel sensitivity to cold air, cold drinks or hot drinks for 1 to 3 days after whitening. It is temporary and harmless.

- Use a toothpaste for sensitive teeth, and brush gently with a soft brush.
- You can leave a little of the toothpaste on your teeth overnight.
- Avoid very cold and very hot drinks for a day or two.
- Over-the-counter pain relief helps if you feel discomfort.
- Call us if the sensitivity is strong or lasts longer than a week.

## Your gums

Your gums may look a little white or feel sore for a few hours. This is a harmless reaction to the gel and fades quickly.

## Take-home trays

If you have take-home whitening trays:

- wear them for the time we showed you, usually 30 to 60 minutes a day, for 10 to 14 days;
- put only a small bead of gel in each tooth space, and wipe away any excess gel from your gums;
- clean the trays with a soft brush and cool water after each use;
- store the trays in their case, away from heat and pets;
- stop for a few days if your teeth become sensitive, then start again with shorter sessions.

## How long do results last?

Results usually last 1 to 3 years, depending on your diet and habits. Coffee, tea, red wine and smoking shorten it. Brush twice a day, floss and have your regular cleaning to keep your teeth bright. You can use a touch-up gel once or twice a year, as we advise.

## Things whitening cannot do

- It does not change the color of fillings, crowns, veneers or bonding. If you have these on front teeth, tell us before whitening, because they may need to be replaced afterwards to match.
- It works less well on gray or brown stains caused by some antibiotics or by injury.
- It is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for children under 16.
